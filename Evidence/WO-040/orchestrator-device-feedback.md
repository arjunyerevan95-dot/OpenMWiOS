# WO-040 — First iPhone gameplay feedback (2026-11-01)

**Disposition:** Accept the user's observation of working gameplay in LiveContainer and the specific held-Attack drag limitation. This supplements the [build review](orchestrator-build-review.md); it does not reopen [WO-040](../../WorkOrders/WO-040.md) or qualify every touch interaction.

- **What was tested:** The user ran the delivered Zink Touch IPA (built source `f4d9f9d8d6bcba1b3bc59201724aab62627aaa4e`) on their iPhone with their Steam Morrowind data. The worker reports verifying all 7,219 transferred files against their source digests. The user reported that the game works well. No game data was included in the two feedback commits.
- **What did not work:** In gameplay, pressing Attack and then sliding **that same finger** does not aim the camera. No failure of aiming with a separate finger was reported. In the shipped [ownership model](https://github.com/arjunyerevan95-dot/OpenMWiOS/blob/f4d9f9d8d6bcba1b3bc59201724aab62627aaa4e/apps/openmw/ios/openmw_ios_touch_model.hpp), a touch starting on Attack is assigned to the button. The [UIKit move handler](https://github.com/arjunyerevan95-dot/OpenMWiOS/blob/f4d9f9d8d6bcba1b3bc59201724aab62627aaa4e/apps/openmw/ios/openmw_ios_touch_controls.mm) sends camera movement for a look touch, not a button touch. This is an input limitation, not evidence of a renderer fault.
- **Review boundary:** The specific Menu, editor, relaunch-persistence and interruption/controller-handoff checks have no individual device results. The user's waived renderer/performance comparison stays waived. The earlier qualified WO33 runtime record is unchanged.
- **Publication:** The [worker report](https://github.com/arjunyerevan95-dot/OpenMWiOS/blob/568323258e0a17f294b91797b68cd29222c374ab/Evidence/WO-040/report.md#device-feedback-after-delivery) is pinned at the verified remote branch tip. The two additions after build handoff (`9758d63ce4`, `568323258e`) change reports only; the [delivered IPA](https://github.com/arjunyerevan95-dot/OpenMWiOS/actions/runs/41200954123/artifacts/13044247922) is unchanged.

If pursued, the narrow follow-up is to keep Attack held while its finger drags to aim, release normally on lift/cancel, and prevent double camera movement if a separate look finger is present. No repair, worker start or new build is authorized here.
