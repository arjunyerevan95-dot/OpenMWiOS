
# DEC-013 — Accept WO33 blend-state reconciliation

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-08-25
- Applies to: WO-033 final acceptance and the qualified OpenMWiOS engineering baseline

## Decision

Accept WO33 as a qualified causal renderer correction. Advance the accepted engineering baseline from `195f3a4bbcfd17ecd46546f3e28d3ee8558bed27` to `93f892dd0cf9834259b4cad2045ddb2ef9c53ed9`.

The accepted cause is an OSG/GL4ES blend-cache desynchronization at OSG's cache gate. On the Apple/manual GL4ES route, OSG could hold a valid cached `GL_BLEND=1` while the GL4ES state used by the target draw remained `blend=0`. The matching OSG cache entry suppressed the GL4ES reconciliation call.

Accept the narrow correction that reasserts only cache-matching `GL_BLEND` requests through `gl4es_glEnable` / `gl4es_glDisable` under Apple + `OPENMW_GL4ES_MANUAL_INIT`. It preserves OSG's cache-match return value and leaves GL4ES's normal state comparison authoritative.

## Evidence accepted

- Amendment 2 diagnostic run `32742643722` successfully built `fd300ba12351252ece9e7b481c0b2d366228f9e8` and produced artifact `OpenMW-iOS-fast-54` / `9528845874`.
- Its 438-record device trace repeatedly showed OSG `requested=1,valid=1,last=1,issued=0` followed by the same-thread GL4ES draw path at `blend=0`, with no active/pending render list.
- Correction run `32771773194` successfully built exact commit `93f892dd0cf9834259b4cad2045ddb2ef9c53ed9` and produced `OpenMW-iOS-fast-55` / `9538962155`.
- The 445-record correction trace contains 55 OSG cache-match reassertions, 55 GL4ES enable-ingress records, and four representative smoke target records at `blend=1` with none at `blend=0`.
- The exact downloaded IPA hashes to `34EB8106C60138F30DA8B59083CA026FDA013F68870DDC658C1584B6E939AC4E`. Its executable hashes to `C759B9D09DF6A2CFFE7657839BD3DB25999A399743E811A7846A406A5826874C` and has Mach-O UUID `FD8BB9B2-AF3F-3CFD-ACAE-B20D1E5D0664`.
- Device images visibly show blended chimney smoke, a composited spell effect, and corrected foliage alpha/cutout presentation while the HUD and exterior scene remain intact. No crash was reported.

## Scope limits

This decision qualifies the smoke/particle blend correction. Foliage transparency is accepted as a visible collateral improvement in this device session, not as proof that every foliage path shares the same cause.

The large white/blank distance regions and sharp blue horizon/cutoff band remain visible, unresolved, and untouched. They become the primary engineering boundary.

Full Qualification did not run. The accepted baseline carries the investigation's bounded renderer diagnostic support and validated 180-minute Fast-build timeout. The diagnostic system is inactive unless `OPENMW_IOS_RENDERER_DIAGNOSTICS=1` and caps its output at 256 KiB; the conditional correction itself made no workflow change.

## Rejected interpretations

- Do not infer that distance/horizon rendering is fixed.
- Do not infer that all foliage behavior has been causally localized.
- Do not generalize this correction into draw-time forced blending or broader state-cache bypass.
- Do not reopen touch, data-path, signing, lifecycle, dependency, or renderer-replacement scope under WO33.
