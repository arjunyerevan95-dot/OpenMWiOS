# DEC-017 — Require a Proven Repeatable Renderer Capture Contract

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-09-19
- Applies to: WO38 closure and WO39 issuance
- Supersedes: DEC-016's prescribed WO37/38 one-shot, observer-only attribution method; retains its accepted facts, rejection of unproven settings remedies, and separate treatment of silhouette/horizon targets

## Decision

Accept WO38 execution/build evidence in part and mark the unfinished order SUPERSEDED. Preserve its existing IPA and source candidates, without claiming device Pause success or renderer attribution. The accepted engineering baseline remains WO33 `93f892dd0cf9834259b4cad2045ddb2ef9c53ed9`.

Issue WO39 READY for bounded source reconstruction, a reusable executable capture-controller prototype, and an implementation-ready observation/reference plan. Do not grant production integration or CI authority until the orchestrator has checked that the proposed measurements can support the desired conclusions. No separate user approval of technical drafting is required; the normal canonical authority gate remains.

## Evidence and rationale

The WO38 observer explicitly lacks depth and direct sky RTT observations requested by the acceptance criteria. Its projected-bounds/center-RGBA, 96-candidate, one-presentation design can narrow hypotheses but is not complete pixel ownership proof. Requiring the user to prepare an exact startup viewpoint does not repair those measurement gaps.

WO33 demonstrated a real OSG/GL4ES state disagreement. WO34–36 ruled out specific settings/radius remedies. Neither result proves the remaining fault is entirely GL4ES, terrain, fog or sky. A complete state/composition model and discriminating observations are needed before selecting a correction boundary.

## Required direction

- Repeatable, explicitly requested capture after the scene is ready; no target-budget consumption during startup.
- Explicit frame/pass/target identities and honest insufficient results.
- Source-verified coverage/depth, effective fog, sky-output and final-composition observations.
- Clearly labeled diagnostic debug views may be designed even if their visualization intentionally differs from ordinary rendering. They must not be mistaken for a product correction or assumed non-perturbing. WO39 does not yet authorize production integration of these views.
- Reusable same-scene references and regression coverage, including retained WO33 blending behavior.
- A coherent renderer-boundary repair if evidence warrants it; no further speculative settings loop or automatic GLES3/fork migration.

Local fixtures prove local contracts, not GPU correctness. Capability gaps must be exposed before another iPhone build, not silently removed from acceptance criteria. Keep investigation proportional: no new general graphics framework, broad tool provisioning, historical re-ingestion or unrelated index migration.
