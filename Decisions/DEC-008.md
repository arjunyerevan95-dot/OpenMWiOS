# DEC-008 — Reject WO31 diagnostic candidate and repair its MRC lifetime boundary

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-08-22
- Applies to: WO-031 original execution and Amendment 1

## Decision

Reject diagnostic source candidate `50aa047c3010be5380393a81c452bcfae206407d` as an engineering baseline. Accept its compact execution evidence as proof that the first WO31 diagnostic build was invalidated by a diagnostic-only Objective-C ownership defect before representative R1/R2 evidence could be collected.

Issue WO31 Amendment 1 as READY. It may correct only that proven lifetime defect, add focused ownership checks, and use one replacement diagnostic Fast Development build before resuming the original bounded renderer investigation. It does not accept or assert any renderer correction.

The accepted engineering source baseline remains `195f3a4bbcfd17ecd46546f3e28d3ee8558bed27`.

## Evidence and causal boundary

- Fast run `32581482760`, artifact `OpenMW-iOS-fast-37` (ID `9478916654`), and the supplied IPS all identify Mach-O UUID `98121EA4-1B76-30BC-83E0-14326772000A`.
- The app launched at `2026-08-22 22:39:03.2436 +0530` and crashed at `22:39:04.0361`, matching the observed sub-second loading-screen failure.
- The exception is main-thread `EXC_BAD_ACCESS` / `SIGSEGV`, `KERN_INVALID_ADDRESS` at `0x20`.
- The decisive stack is `objc_retain` → `NSDictionary` construction → `openmw_ios_renderer_diag_record` → `Shader::ShaderVisitor::createProgram`.
- The diagnostic file contains only its startup record. The failure therefore occurred while constructing the first later renderer record.
- In non-ARC `ios/openmw_ios_renderer_diagnostics.mm`, `sPath` and `sSession` were raw persistent `NSString *` globals assigned autoreleased values inside the startup `@autoreleasepool`. Their use after that pool drained is invalid. The stack proves the session value faulted during dictionary construction; the path has the same source-level lifetime defect.

This is sufficient to establish the diagnostic regression without a dSYM because the artifact identity matches and the relevant app/renderer frames are symbolicated.

## Non-conclusions

This decision does not establish:

- the earliest invalid R1 alpha/particle boundary;
- the earliest invalid R2 distance/fog/horizon boundary;
- that R1 and R2 converge;
- a signing, provisioning, entitlement, dyld, SideStore, or installation fault;
- authority for wider lifecycle, build-system, touch, or renderer changes.

## Consequences

- The original WO31 run remains stopped under Condition F.
- Its worker evidence is preserved, but its source candidate is not merged into the accepted baseline.
- Amendment 1 requires a fresh explicit start directive before execution.
- The replacement artifact must first reach menu/world runtime and write startup plus later OpenMW and GL4ES records without this crash.
- Only then may the original R1/R2 causal investigation resume.
