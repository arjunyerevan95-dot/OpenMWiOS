# DEC-012 — Accept WO32 falsification and reject the OSG blend-routing correction

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-08-24
- Applies to: WO-032 final review and WO-033 issuance

## Decision

Accept WO32 as a partial diagnostic and falsification result. Accept its build, artifact, and device evidence. Reject correction candidate `1625713b949ddb0cde5471feead75f29fedadfaa` as insufficient: the exact source compiled and ran, but GL4ES still recorded `blend=0` for every representative chimney-smoke draw and the visible smoke remained blocky.

Do not advance the qualified runtime/source baseline. It remains `195f3a4bbcfd17ecd46546f3e28d3ee8558bed27`.

Close WO32 as ACCEPTED for evidence only and issue WO33 as READY to trace the actual blend-state transition from OSG decision through GL4ES ingress/context/render-list execution to the target draw. No further correction is authorized until that sequence proves the first invalid transition.

## Accepted facts

- Fast run `32649331052` successfully built exact head `c9161e579ff1814cc7fc5c86fb63a9ef177502f3` and uploaded `OpenMW-iOS-fast-51`.
- The OSG correction and its focused test are byte-for-byte identical between correction commit `1625713b949ddb0cde5471feead75f29fedadfaa` and the built commit.
- The exact device IPA reached the mandatory Seyda Neen smoke view and produced a fresh bounded diagnostic file.
- Four correlated draws for `textures/tx_smokealpha00a.dds` all contain correct alpha factors, disabled depth writes, fragment discard/alpha output, and `blend=0`.
- The matching screenshot shows no material improvement.
- No second build, speculative adjustment, Full Qualification, touch work, foliage work, or R2 work occurred.

## Rejected inference

WO32 previously established a state-ownership mismatch, but it did not prove that the two patched OSG mode sites were the complete production transition path. Device evidence now proves that routing those sites is not sufficient. It does not establish whether:

- the patched OSG branch is compiled and exercised for the representative draw;
- GL4ES receives an enable which is later disabled;
- OSG and the target draw use different GL4ES context/state objects;
- GL4ES render-list batching or replay restores another state snapshot;
- another state owner bypasses or overwrites the tracked value.

These are ordered diagnostic alternatives, not accepted causes.

## Operational build fact

The timeout-only change from 120 to 180 minutes allowed the cold correction build to complete in 1h21m13s. That workflow edit is useful execution evidence but is not accepted into the qualified product baseline. WO33 may preserve it on its investigation continuation branch to avoid repeating the prior timeout boundary.
