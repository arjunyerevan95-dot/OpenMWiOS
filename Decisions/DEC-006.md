# DEC-006 — Maximum View Distance Does Not Resolve Exterior Rendering Defects

Status: established

## Decision / finding

Maximum OpenMW view distance improves exterior scene coverage somewhat but does not resolve the observed Seyda Neen rendering defects. A low view-distance setting is not a complete explanation.

The durable symptoms are:

- foliage and hanging-moss planes rendered as dark/opaque cards instead of alpha-cutout vegetation;
- large white/blank distance regions;
- disconnected or abruptly clipped distant silhouettes;
- a sharp blue horizontal horizon/cutoff band.

Foliage alpha/cutout and distant scene/fog/horizon behavior remain separate investigative boundaries until evidence proves convergence.

## Applies to

- Scoping a future renderer investigation.
- Preventing view-distance configuration from being treated as a complete correction.
- Keeping foliage-alpha and distant-rendering hypotheses independently testable.

## Does NOT establish

- A root cause in OpenMW, OSG, GL4ES, shaders, fog, culling/paging, clipping, clear color, or configuration.
- That foliage and distance defects share one cause.
- That water is currently defective; its appearance improved when shader effects were enabled.
- Authorization to change renderer, graphics configuration, or touch UX.

## Supersedes

Any informal interpretation that increasing view distance alone resolves the white/empty distance presentation. No durable renderer decision is otherwise superseded.
