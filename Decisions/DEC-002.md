# DEC-002 — Touch Traversal Is Viable but Android Fidelity Is Incomplete

Status: supported

## Decision / finding

The current iOS touch foundation is sufficient for movement and camera look through the prison ship and into Seyda Neen exterior, while preserving rendering and scene transitions. It is not yet a faithful or complete port of the recent Android control layout/action behavior.

## Why it matters

Future work should refine the existing touch foundation and action bridge rather than restarting the control architecture. At the same time, the partial result must not be represented as complete touch qualification.

## Evidence

- Implementation commit `8845e38349a356bb8dc3e496723a93c7ea39bf52`.
- Qualified compile-fix commit `5c60db4c4f1ca5c135a0bea754496b7a2f1af3f1`.
- Fast Development run `32468623142` (success).
- [WO-026](../WorkOrders/WO-026.md) device observation: working movement/look and traversal to Seyda Neen.
- Inventory mismatch: current iOS action injects keyboard `B`, while the active OpenMW binding expects right-mouse/controller `B` behavior.

## Applies to

- Future iOS control-layout, input-action, and GUI/gameplay-mode work.

## Does NOT establish

- Full Android visual/layout fidelity.
- Correct behavior of every displayed action button.
- Complete simultaneous movement + look + third-button qualification.
- Exterior performance or exterior renderer correctness.

## Supersedes

The prior state in which exterior testing was blocked because the player could not traverse the intro through touch input.
