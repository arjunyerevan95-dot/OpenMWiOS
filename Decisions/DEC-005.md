# DEC-005 — Accept the WO29 Touch-Icon Cache Ownership Correction

Status: established

## Decision / finding

Accept correction `45c1d8c696fb328d977893b79efc78689a3f4d08` as the current engineering baseline for the WO28 crash boundary.

The rejected WO28 launch-time, `New Game`, and overlay-action crash observations converge at one proven mechanism: `OpenMWTouchOverlay` cached autoreleased `UIImage` pointers in a process-lifetime raw-pointer array while `openmw_ios_touch_controls.mm` was compiled without ARC. Later redraws messaged dangling or reused addresses.

The correction constructs owned images with `[[UIImage alloc] initWithData:]` and releases the temporary `NSData`. It does not change touch mappings, layout, renderer, data path, dependencies, cache architecture, or workflow behavior.

## Evidence

- Thirteen device reports match rejected executable UUID `FD8A7812-C514-3995-BB1C-08F921EBCD7B`.
- All thirteen fault at `-[OpenMWTouchOverlay drawRect:] + 0x35c` while sending `imageWithTintColor:renderingMode:` to a stale/reused receiver.
- Five launch, three `New Game`, and five menu/overlay-context reports share the signature.
- [Crash classification](../Evidence/WO-029/crash-classification.md).
- [WO29 evidence manifest](../Evidence/WO-029/manifest.md).
- Focused touch tests passed: 19 passed, one compiler-dependent skip.
- Fast Development run `32548876888` succeeded at correction commit `45c1d8c`.
- Artifact `OpenMW-iOS-fast-31`, ID `9470501854`; IPA and executable hashes were independently reverified.
- The corrected device session booted normally and exercised controls, indoor/exterior gameplay, traversal, HUD/overlay, weapon/combat, and scene changes without a user-observed native crash, unexplained termination, or jetsam.

## Acceptance limit

The formal ten-launch, three-`New Game`, and per-control attempt counts were not recorded, and an independent post-test crash inventory was unavailable. Acceptance qualifies the proven correction and observed sustained device pass; it does not establish complete mobile-control fidelity or invent the missing counts.

The corrected uploaded artifact did not contain a dSYM or link map. That does not invalidate the rejected-candidate causal finding, which used exact UUID-matched reports, function-level frames, selector/receiver evidence, compile mode, and checked-out source.

## SideStore context

SideStore re-signing changed the installed bundle identifier but did not change the rejected executable's Mach-O UUID. No report implicates code signing, entitlements, dyld, provisioning, or installation state as the crash cause.

## Applies to

- The accepted WO29 crash correction.
- The current OpenMWiOS engineering baseline.
- Future touch-overlay image lifetime and non-ARC ownership work.

## Does NOT establish

- Complete mobile-control qualification.
- Acceptance of icon opacity, resizing, repositioning, or customization.
- A renderer correction or relationship to exterior graphics defects.
- General safety of unrelated Objective-C++ ownership paths.

## Supersedes

The provisional separation requirement in [DEC-004](DEC-004.md) for these three crash observations. UUID-matched crash evidence now proves their convergence at the cached-image ownership defect. DEC-004's rejection of the WO28 candidate and its environmental caution remain valid.
