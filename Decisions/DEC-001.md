# DEC-001 — Preserve the Qualified Renderer and Runtime Baseline

Status: established

## Decision / finding

The current iOS renderer path is a qualified baseline: SDL/UIKit presentation, OSG routed through GL4ES, the external drawable mapping, full-screen aspect correction, shader compatibility patches, and OpenMW resource/VFS corrections together render the main menu and a textured, lit 3D world on device. Future control or data-path work must not reopen these layers without contradictory evidence.

## Why it matters

Earlier failures were isolated and corrected across multiple work orders. WO24 qualified the recognizable 3D world; WO25A qualified native full-screen landscape rendering; WO26 and WO27 preserved that result through exterior traversal and runtime data-path changes.

## Evidence

- WO24 fragment-link qualification and device result, summarized in the living ledger.
- Commit `cf1d2fd1521bc4465709a56f1a387637d474b56f` and WO25A device evidence for full-screen native aspect.
- [WO-026](../WorkOrders/WO-026.md): intact rendering, UI, audio, and transitions through exterior traversal.
- [WO-027](../WorkOrders/WO-027.md): intact full-screen textured/lit Jiub scene after the data-path correction.

## Applies to

- Future touch, lifecycle, configuration, data-location, and product-quality work.
- Regression review of GL4ES, OSG, SDL/UIKit presentation, VFS/MWUI, and shader changes.

## Does NOT establish

- Correct foliage alpha/transparency in exterior scenes.
- Correct distant rendering or water settings.
- Exterior performance qualification.
- Correctness of render scale 0.58.
- That `ColorMaski` warnings are harmless in every pass.

## Supersedes

Earlier hypotheses that the port could not reach a live, textured, lit game world or full-screen iPhone presentation.
