# DEC-016 — Accept WO36 falsification and require exact distant-pixel attribution

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-08-26
- Applies to: WO-036 final review and WO-037 issuance

## Decision

Accept WO36 as a controlled same-binary falsification. Enabling distant terrain/object paging at `7168` does not materially change the exterior blue band, fog-colored/white uncovered region, or dark silhouettes. Increasing the camera, fog, and view distance to `16384` improves moderate-distance coverage but only displaces the same boundary farther away and carries a modest observed performance cost.

Do not change the product's distant-terrain or view-distance defaults. Do not advance the accepted engineering baseline; it remains `93f892dd0cf9834259b4cad2045ddb2ef9c53ed9`.

Issue WO37 as READY to correlate representative target pixels with exact OSG draw identity, GL4ES program/state, sky RTT sampling, depth, and scene clear/background behavior. Treat black silhouettes and the blue/white horizon boundary as distinct until the correlated evidence proves convergence.

## Accepted facts

- Evidence commit `4f6cfc62ced645d4465f401520a1dfaca5e65ab7` is evidence-only and based on the accepted engineering baseline.
- Arm A proves `view_distance=7168` and `object_paging=0`.
- Arm B proves `view_distance=7168` and `object_paging=1`; the target remains.
- Arm C proves `selected=16384`, `camera_far=16384`, fog/view end `16384`, `terrain_cull=27934.2`, and `object_paging=1`.
- Six independently verified Arm C captures show improved moderate-distance coverage but the same defects at the farther boundary.
- Exact Arm A configuration was restored after testing.
- No source, build, workflow, CI run, artifact, or worker-authored canonical change occurred.

## Rejected directions

The following are not authorized as renderer corrections on present evidence:

- enabling distant terrain by default;
- raising the iOS default view distance above `7168`;
- another terrain-radius expansion;
- another global fog/sky-blending toggle; or
- treating a larger blank-free radius as a fixed rendering boundary.

A user may select a higher distance locally if they prefer the extra radius and accept its performance cost. That local preference is not qualified product behavior.

## Next causal boundary

Existing diagnostics prove camera/fog intent and broad program receipt but do not say which drawable/program produced a photographed black silhouette or whether a photographed white/blue pixel had any scene geometry at all. Pinned-source inspection establishes a viable non-mutating attribution seam:

- OSG `RenderLeaf::render` owns the final drawable, model-view/projection matrices, render depth, and call to `Drawable::draw`;
- GL4ES `glDrawElementsCommon` owns the realized program and draw-time state immediately before the GLES draw; and
- OpenMW's compatibility shaders own fog and optional sky RTT sampling.

WO37 must join these boundaries with bounded diagnostic-only correlation before permitting a correction.
