## Decision

Accept the Amendment 1 evidence that explicit MRC ownership repaired the WO31 diagnostic launch crash. Do not accept the replacement artifact as a qualified engineering or renderer baseline because it produced no GL4ES/R2 diagnostic records and showed an unclassified short-tap Menu regression.

Issue WO31 Amendment 2 as READY. It remains renderer-focused and authorizes one additional diagnostic build only after source/linkage tests prove that the production app can emit a GL4ES handshake and R2 records. The final renderer-correction build remains reserved for a correction supported by representative intended/applied device evidence.

The accepted engineering baseline remains `195f3a4bbcfd17ecd46546f3e28d3ee8558bed27`. No renderer correction is accepted.

## Interpretation

The attached JSONL is sufficient evidence of partial diagnostic operation and of Stop Condition B. “Zero GL4ES/R2 records” does not mean the user supplied the wrong file or an empty file. It means the current probe/correlation/call-site design did not observe the lower renderer state needed to locate the defect.

The absence has two separate investigative boundaries:

1. GL4ES availability/linkage and R1 correlation must be proven independently of texture-content matching. The present path only writes GL4ES R1 records after a registered OpenMW texture hash matches GL4ES upload bytes.
2. R2 probe call sites must be proven to execute in the tested exterior session. Silence is not evidence that fog is disabled or correctly applied.

## Menu disposition

Preserve the short-tap Menu observation as an unclassified candidate regression. Because the candidate contains no touch-source change and the user prioritizes renderer work, it does not authorize touch implementation or a diversion from renderer observability. Amendment 2 must recheck it once. A repeated failure blocks final candidate acceptance but does not invalidate already collected renderer evidence.

## Consequences

- Amendment 1 remains stopped under Conditions B and F.
- Worker evidence is preserved without merging its diagnostic source into the accepted baseline.
- Amendment 2 requires an explicit start directive.
- One additional diagnostic build is authorized only after local and linked-product proof covers both app/OpenMW and GL4ES emission.
- If that build still lacks the required GL4ES/R1/R2 chains, WO31 stops without another redesign build.
