# DEC-014 — Accept WO34 falsification and reject the terrain-radius correction

- Status: **ACCEPTED**
- Date: 2026-08-25
- Applies to: WO-034 final review and WO-035 issuance

## Decision

Accept WO34 as a controlled source experiment and device falsification. Accept its execution evidence, exact CI/artifact identity, and disciplined stop. Reject terrain-radius correction `398a91759b1b327b464cf81e41320b8e5f312c9b` as a product candidate because the exact device build produced no material change to the white/fog-colored regions, blue cutoff band, or black distant silhouettes.

Do not advance the accepted engineering baseline. It remains `93f892dd0cf9834259b4cad2045ddb2ef9c53ed9`.

Close WO34 as ACCEPTED for evidence/falsification only. Issue WO35 as READY to A/B test OpenMW's existing sky-blending feature before authorizing another renderer diagnostic or correction.

## Accepted facts

- WO34's explicit start directive was delivered and acknowledged, although the separate canonical ACTIVE bookkeeping commit was missed.
- Correction commit `398a91759b1b327b464cf81e41320b8e5f312c9b` is a direct child of the prescribed worker checkpoint.
- Fast run `32854124142` successfully built that exact commit and produced artifact `OpenMW-iOS-fast-56` / `9568728950`.
- The independently downloaded IPA hashes to `EE9631158A008A2304A3CA766F2DDD9BFBDA2DD9ED6A9775297DE9FC3DEDB043`.
- All seven device screenshot identities match the manifest and visibly retain the target defects.
- No second build or Full Qualification ran, and no canonical files were changed by the worker.

## Rejected inference

The aspect-blind formula does not enclose the full mathematical perspective frustum, but WO34 does not qualify the larger full-corner radius as a useful OpenMWiOS product change. The observed renderer defects did not respond, and the larger traversal radius carries unmeasured cost. Do not retain or build upon patch `0016-ios-cover-perspective-frustum-terrain.patch` without a new independent requirement and authorization.

The falsification does not prove active-grid, fog-coordinate, shader/program, sky/clear, depth, or asset behavior correct. It only removes terrain-radius expansion as the cause of these device-visible symptoms.

## Next bounded hypothesis

The accepted WO33 trace records scene-camera `sky_blending=0`. Direct pinned-source verification establishes that:

- OpenMW's shipped default is `sky blending = false`;
- its own setting documentation describes sky blending as the mechanism for hiding the clipping plane;
- `RenderingManager` uses the setting to enable the shader define, sky RTT, sky texture, and per-view uniforms; and
- the accepted screenshots visibly contain a sharp clipping-plane-like sky/world boundary.

This evidence authorizes a configuration-only A/B test. It does not yet prove that sky blending will correct the iOS presentation, and it does not assume the black silhouettes share the blue/white cutoff cause.
