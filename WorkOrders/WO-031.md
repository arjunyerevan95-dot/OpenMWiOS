## Objective

Correct the real, reproducible exterior rendering defects visible around Seyda Neen:

1. **R1 — alpha/particle presentation**
   - opaque foliage and hanging-moss cards;
   - blocky rectangular spell-fire layers and chimney-smoke particles.
2. **R2 — distance/fog/horizon presentation**
   - large white/blank distance regions;
   - disconnected or abruptly clipped distant silhouettes;
   - the sharp blue horizontal horizon/cutoff band and absent/incoherent distance fog.

This is a renderer-only order. The accepted WO30 touch editor, opacity behavior, layout, and input mapping are baseline and must not be changed. Menu/options scrolling and minor touch-editor contract gaps are explicitly deferred.

- Increasing OpenMW view distance to maximum improves coverage but does not remove the R1 or R2 defects (DEC-006).
- WO30 verified intended source paths through OpenMW/NIF/OSG/GL4ES but did not capture a representative exterior R1 state record or the GL4ES half of R1/R2 on the user-shareable path.
- WO30 recorded OpenMW intended exterior fog start `2007.04`, end/far `7168`, color `0.960784,0.921569,0.878431`, underwater `0`; it did not prove what GL4ES received or consumed.
- The shared `openmw.log` contained about 8.86 million `ColorMaski` warnings plus GL errors. Temporal association with transparent/render-bin draws is relevant but is not causal proof.
- Foliage cards plus rectangular fire/smoke broaden the R1 symptom family and make shared alpha/blend state a priority hypothesis, not an established cause.
- R1 and R2 remain separate until correlated runtime state proves convergence.
- Codebase Memory can omit Objective-C++ bridge functions; direct source remains authoritative.

## Acceptance criteria

WO31 succeeds when:

1. The device-shareable evidence correlates OpenMW/OSG intended state with GL4ES received/applied state for representative R1 and R2 samples.
2. Each corrected path has an evidence-backed earliest invalid boundary and a minimal deterministic correction with focused tests.
3. Foliage/hanging moss preserve transparent cutouts and qualified fire/smoke particles no longer render as unexplained rectangular layers.
4. Qualified exterior views no longer show the large white/blank regions or sharp blue cutoff band, and distance fog/sky/horizon presentation is coherent at both view-distance settings.
5. R1 and R2 are kept separate unless evidence proves a common cause.
6. Indoor/UI/HUD/water/native-aspect/data-path/touch baselines show no regression or crash in the supplied matrix.
7. CI budget, diagnostic budgets, prohibited changes, and evidence requirements are met.

A source-backed correction of only R1 or only R2 is a valid **PARTIAL / STOPPED** result. An investigation without a proven correction is evidence, not success, and must stop without a speculative workaround.

## Stop conditions

- **A — Control conflict:** baseline, authorization, canonical ownership, or concurrent CI/work is inconsistent.
- **B — Diagnostic channel insufficient:** the one diagnostic build cannot correlate representative device state. Preserve evidence and stop.
- **C — Cause unproven:** R1 or R2 earliest invalid boundary remains unknown. Do not guess.
- **D — Dependency revision/broad migration required:** stop with source evidence.
- **E — New boundary or scope collision:** evidence requires touch changes, unrelated renderer work, or a prohibited subsystem. Stop for orchestrator review.
- **F — Regression/crash:** accepted runtime, touch, data, indoor/UI/water, or lifecycle behavior regresses. Capture and stop.
- **G — Build budget exhausted:** do not trigger another run.
- **H — Qualified success or valid partial correction:** report and stop for orchestrator review.
