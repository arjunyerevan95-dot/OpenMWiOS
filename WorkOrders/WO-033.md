## Established facts

- The exact WO32 correction source compiled and ran; stale/wrong-source IPA is excluded.
- `textures/tx_smokealpha00a.dds` reaches program 9 as a translucent DXT5 target with standard alpha factors, disabled depth writes, and fragment discard/alpha output.
- Four corrected-candidate draws still record `blend=0`; visible smoke remains blocky.
- GL4ES `gl4es_glEnable` and `gl4es_glDisable` call `proxy_glEnable`; its `GL_BLEND` case mutates `glstate->enable.blend` when the requested value differs.
- The target draw diagnostic reads `glstate->enable.blend` in `glDrawElementsCommon` on the actual draw path.
- GL4ES can batch/intercept work through render lists before common draw execution.
- Source and patch presence do not prove that a conditional branch was compiled or exercised for the target draw.
