## Objective

Localize and minimally correct the remaining exterior rendering defects without another broad startup probe pass:

1. **R1 — foliage and chimney smoke:** capture the applied GL4ES draw/program state for dynamically identified defective foliage and visible chimney-smoke textures, then correct the first proven alpha/blend/depth/shader divergence.
2. **R2 — distance, fog, and horizon:** capture exterior program fog consumption and the relevant camera/content/cull/sky/clear/depth state at two materially different viewing distances, then correct the first proven boundary producing blank/white distance regions or the sharp blue horizon band.

A deliberately cast fire spell is optional. Visible chimney smoke is the required representative particle case. Touch behavior is out of scope.

### Phase 1 — target-gated R1 sampling

1. Arm draw capture only after a dynamically classified defective asset is registered and bound to its current GL texture name.
2. Record only draws whose currently bound texture maps to a qualified foliage or particle identity/category. Do not spend budget on `category=-1` or unrelated early UI/control draws.
3. Required cases:
   - one visibly defective translucent foliage/moss/fern draw;
   - one visibly defective chimney-smoke draw;
   - one known-correct opaque control draw captured under the same exterior program family.
4. For each, record texture identity/format/alpha classification, OSG alpha/material state, GL4ES program, blend factors, alpha-test emulation, depth test/write, render ordering, shader defines, fragment alpha behavior, and draw count.
5. Emit a bounded target-hit/target-miss summary so absence is distinguishable from budget exhaustion.
6. Fire-spell casting is optional and must not be required for acceptance if chimney smoke is captured.

### Phase 2 — post-receipt R2 sampling

1. Arm applied-program capture only after the exterior OpenMW/OSG intent is active and GL4ES has received the matching fog state.
2. Record the representative exterior programs/draws after receipt, including whether fog code/uniforms are present and the exact consumed start/end/color/scale.
3. At two materially different view-distance settings from the same exterior viewpoint, record:
   - selected/effective view distance and camera near/far projection;
   - loaded cell/terrain/paging coverage and cull range;
   - fog intent, OSG application, GL4ES receipt, and program consumption;
   - sky/background/clear color and depth behavior at the visible white/blue boundary.
4. If exterior program consumption is correct, continue within this phase to identify whether the first bad boundary is content loading, culling/clipping, sky/clear presentation, or depth state. Do not stop at “fog values match.”
5. Emit bounded state-change records rather than repeated identical per-frame records.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A defective foliage draw and a defective chimney-smoke draw are correlated from asset identity through OSG state to applied GL4ES draw/program state.
2. A correct exterior control draw is captured for comparison.
3. R2 is correlated through OpenMW intent, OSG application, GL4ES receipt, and exterior program consumption at two view distances.
