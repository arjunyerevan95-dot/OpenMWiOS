## Objective

Identify the exact render boundary behind representative exterior defects, then apply a narrow correction only if the diagnostic evidence proves one:

1. abrupt near-black distant object/terrain/tree silhouettes, including objects that fade normally at a similar apparent distance while others remain black; and
2. the sharp blue horizontal band and large pale/white fog-colored uncovered region at the exterior horizon.

Treat these as distinct paths until correlated evidence proves they converge. The required distinction is:

- **drawn-pixel failure** — scene geometry covers the target pixel but uses an invalid shader/program, fog/sky sample, texture, lighting, blend, depth, cull, or uniform state; versus
- **coverage/composition failure** — no world drawable covers the target pixel, leaving scene clear color or a sky/RTT boundary visible.

Do not apply another global distance, paging, terrain-radius, fog-enable, or sky-blending change. Existing WO34–WO36 evidence has already falsified those as corrections.

## Established evidence and source boundary

### Accepted runtime facts

- Camera/fog/view distance `7168` versus `16384` changes where the defects appear, not their character.
- `object_paging 0 → 1` at constant `7168` does not remove them.
- Sky blending is proven active in the retained device configuration and improves some fading, but does not remove either target.
- Accepted WO33 diagnostics prove broad OpenMW fog intent, GL4ES receipt, and program application, but they do not correlate a photographed target pixel to a specific drawable or program.
- The scene clear color recorded in the WO36 Arm C session matches the pale fog-colored background, but target-pixel depth/coverage was not sampled.

### Source-verified attribution seams

Codebase Memory reconnaissance followed by direct inspection of the pinned checked-out source establishes:

- OSG `osgUtil::RenderLeaf::render` has the final `Drawable`, model-view and projection matrices, render depth, state graph, and the immediate call to `Drawable::draw`.
- OSG `Drawable::draw` is the common drawable execution boundary before geometry-specific draw implementation.
- GL4ES `glDrawElementsCommon` realizes textures and holds the active GL4ES program/state immediately before the actual GLES draw.
- OpenMW `applyFogAtDist`/`applyFogAtPos` owns fog mixing and optional `sampleSkyColor(gl_FragCoord.xy / screenRes)` behavior.
- OpenMW's sky RTT is a bounded offscreen texture, while the scene camera separately clears to the live fog color.

The worker may choose a narrower verified seam, but any diagnostic design must join draw identity to actual GL4ES program/state and target-pixel coverage. Names or graph results alone are insufficient.

### Phase 1 — causal model and observer-only diagnostic design

Design the smallest environment-gated diagnostic that can correlate a saved target view with both OSG and GL4ES without changing rendered output.

For a screen-center/crosshair target, the capture must record or prove, where applicable:

1. session/frame/draw correlation identifiers that survive OSG → GL4ES;
2. drawable and nearest useful parent/node identity, without relying on a hard-coded asset;
3. object-space bounds plus projected screen/NDC coverage, eye depth/distance, and render bin/order;
4. bound diffuse texture identity/hash when available;
5. realized GL4ES program identity and whether the fragment path contains/uses fog and sky sampling;
6. fog start/end/color/scale, far distance, sky-blending start, screen resolution, sky sampler unit/binding, and relevant program uniform locations/values;
7. blend, alpha, depth-test/write/function, cull, and material/lighting state needed to distinguish a black output from expected fog/sky mixing;
8. target-pixel final color and depth after scene rendering; and
9. scene clear color plus the sky RTT color sampled at the same normalized screen coordinate, if the path uses sky blending.

The diagnostic must explicitly classify:

- no drawable covered the target pixel / clear or sky composition exposed;
- one or more candidate drawables covered it, with the winning/nearest draw identified; or
- attribution remained ambiguous.

Use saved-camera first exterior frames, a compact ring/filter, or another source-verified trigger so startup traffic cannot exhaust the budget before the target. A diagnostic-only request file or lifecycle trigger is allowed if environment-gated and documented. Do not modify touch controls, Menu behavior, HUD layout, or gameplay input to trigger capture.
