## Objective

Determine whether OpenMW's existing sky-blending feature removes the sharp blue clipping-plane band and fog-colored/white exterior cutoff on the physical iPhone. Prove or falsify that hypothesis first with a same-binary configuration A/B test. Only if it visibly passes may the worker make sky blending the iOS default on the accepted engineering baseline and produce one qualified Fast build.

Treat the pure-black distant silhouettes as a potentially distinct path. A partial result that fixes the blue/white cutoff but leaves black silhouettes is valid and must not be broadened into another speculative correction.

## Established evidence

### WO34 falsification

WO34 increased the captured terrain coverage radius from about `8276.89` to `12221.21`, built the exact change, and tested it on the physical device. The white/fog-colored regions, sharp blue band, and black silhouettes were materially unchanged. Terrain-radius expansion is rejected as the cause and must not be retained.

### Sky-blending state and source behavior

The accepted WO33 device trace, session `9504FA5C-CF45-46D9-908E-2F1838B8B0B9`, records:

```text
family=r2.clear
clear_color=0.960784,0.921569,0.878431,1
clear_mask=17664
clear_depth=1
sky_blending=0
```

Direct inspection of pinned OpenMW `f4bec41444214a7903bebd178389ca22ca13f646` establishes:

- `files/settings-default.cfg` ships `sky blending = false` and `sky blending start = 0.8`;
- OpenMW documents this feature as blending distant presentation with the sky to hide the clipping plane;
- `RenderingManager` reads the setting at construction, sets the `skyBlending` shader define, creates the sky RTT path, reserves the sky texture, and enables the per-view sky sampler only when enabled; and
- `applyFogAtDist` blends scene color with the sampled sky from `skyBlendingStart` to the far distance.

This is a strong configuration hypothesis, not an accepted cause. It must pass an exact device A/B before any product default changes.

## Acceptance criteria

WO35 succeeds fully when:

1. a same-binary physical-device A/B proves sky blending materially removes or smooths the blue/white clipping-plane presentation;
2. the production change is limited to a user-overridable iOS default;
3. rejected WO34 terrain code is absent;
4. the exact product compiles, links, validates, packages, and yields a verified IPA;
5. the correction IPA activates `sky_blending=1` without a manual key;
6. matching device views retain the Phase 1 improvement;
7. nearby scene, water, HUD, touch, smoke, foliage, and spell rendering remain intact; and
8. no crash or obvious severe performance/memory regression occurs.

A valid partial success is that the blue band and fog-colored/white cutoff materially improve while black distant silhouettes remain. That outcome must close this work order and leave silhouettes as the next separate renderer boundary.
